# Demo Home policies

Fictional policies for a portfolio demonstration. Version 1.

## P1 — Damage reporting window
Damage must be reported within 14 calendar days after delivery. The delivery date is day 0; day 14 is included. Use the date of the complaint, not the date it is processed. A report before delivery requires human review.

## P2 — Photo evidence
A damage photo is required before a timely claim qualifies for a free replacement. If no photo is provided, request one. If its availability is unknown, ask. The demo tracks photo availability; it does not inspect images or establish whether evidence is authentic.

## P3 — Unknown orders
If the order is not found, ask the customer to verify the number and mark the case for human review. An unsuccessful lookup does not prove customer error. Do not approve or reject the claim until the order is verified.

## P4 — Late claims
Claims reported after day 14 do not qualify under the standard free replacement policy. A human may review the case, but no exception or alternative remedy is promised.

## P5 — Authority and missing information
The assistant drafts recommendations only. Employees authorize and perform actions. Missing, contradictory, or unsupported information must be clarified or referred for human review. No contact or handoff takes place through this demo.
